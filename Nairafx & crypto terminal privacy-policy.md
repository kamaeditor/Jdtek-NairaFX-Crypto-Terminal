# Privacy Policy for JDTEK Market Terminal

**Effective Date:** June 21, 2026

JDTEK DIGITAL Ltd ("we," "us," or "our") operates the **JDTEK Market Terminal** mobile application. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data and the security measures we take to protect your privacy.

### 1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information to provide and improve our Service:

* **Personal Data:** For account authentication and synchronization, we use Firebase Authentication. This may involve your email address to secure your user profile.
* **Usage Data:** We collect data on how the Service is accessed, including device IP addresses, device types, and app interaction logs (such as clicks on specific FX pairs or listings) via Firebase Analytics.
* **Market Data:** The app fetches currency and market data from our secure servers. This is a general broadcast; we do not collect your individual identity data to provide these informational updates.

### 2. Third-Party Service Providers & Browser Security

To provide a seamless "Market Terminal" experience, we use trusted third-party services:

* **Firebase (Google):** We use Firestore for data storage, Authentication for security, and Cloud Messaging (FCM) for real-time market alerts.
* **Google AdMob:** We display advertisements. AdMob may collect device-level identifiers (AD-ID) to serve relevant ads. You can opt-out of personalized ads in your Android device settings.
* **Android Custom Tabs:** To interact with third-party portals, we utilize Android Custom Tabs. This ensures that your browser sessions (Discord, Twitter, or Wallet logins) are isolated. We do not access, store, or see any credentials you enter on these third-party sites.

### 3. Financial Services & Blockchain Disclosure

In compliance with Google Play's 2026 Financial Services policies:

* **Informational Only:** **JDTEK Market Terminal** is an informational tool. We do not provide financial advice, brokerage services, or crypto exchange functionality.
* **Blockchain Content:** We provide tracking for tokenized digital assets. We do not provide crypto wallet services; all external blockchain interactions occur outside the app via the user's chosen browser.

### 4. Data Safety & Sharing

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety requirements:

* **Data Collection:** We collect Device IDs and Crash Logs for app functionality, stability, and advertising.
* **Data Sharing:** We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with our service providers (Firebase/AdMob) solely to operate and monetize the app.
* **Encryption:** All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.

### 5. Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) Compliance

As a Nigerian-based tech firm, we respect your rights under the NDPR:

* **Right to Access/Delete:** You may request access to or deletion of your authentication data at any time.
* **Consent:** By using the app, you consent to the processing of your data as outlined here.
* **Data Minimization:** We only collect the minimum data necessary to provide market rates and tracking.

### 6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

### 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy to reflect app improvements or regulatory changes. We will notify you by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
